Privacy Policy – Tracing School

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android app Tracing School (package com.tracing.school.learning) handles information. The app is designed for children and also used by older learners, parents and teachers. We follow the Google Play Families Policy.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, store or share any personal information. The app has no sign-up, no login, no chat and no in-app purchases. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts or location.

2. Tracing content

The tracing lessons are loaded from our website (dijolapp.github.io/Tracing-School). Your tracing is not uploaded or saved by us.

3. Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob to keep it free. To protect children:

  • All ad requests are marked as child-directed (COPPA and Families Policy).
  • Only ads rated G (suitable for all audiences) are shown.
  • Ads are not personalised and are not based on interests or behaviour.
  • The app does not access the Android Advertising ID.
  • Sensitive ad categories (such as dating, gambling, alcohol and politics) are blocked.

To deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google AdMob may automatically collect limited technical information, such as device type, operating system version, app interactions with ads, an app-set identifier, crash and diagnostic data, and approximate location derived from the IP address. This data is handled by Google under its own policy: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). The only third-party SDK in the app that handles data is Google AdMob, which is certified for use in apps for children under the Google Play Families Policy and is configured for child-directed treatment. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

5. Data security

All data sent by the app and by Google AdMob is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

6. Your choices

You can stop all data collection by uninstalling the app. You can also reset or delete your device's advertising settings in Android Settings → Google → Ads.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

8. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, email us at dijol.app@gmail.com.
